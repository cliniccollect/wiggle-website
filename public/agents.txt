# Wiggle

> Wiggle is a point-of-sale and payment platform for veterinary clinics. Clinic staff use it to take payments in the clinic and online, and pet owners use it to pay their bills.

Wiggle is made by Wiggle Technology Inc. Website: https://wiggle.vet

## Who it is for

- Veterinary clinics and hospitals, both small animal and equine.
- Clinic staff: front desk, practice managers and veterinarians.
- Pet owners, who are the clinic's clients and pay through Wiggle.

## What it does

- Card payments in the clinic on payment terminals.
- Integrates with the clinic's practice management system (PIMS) for one-click checkout. Payments post back to the PIMS automatically.
- Payment links sent to clients by text message or email, so clients can pay from their phone.
- Online bill pay, so pet owners can pay their clinic balance online at any time.
- Saved cards on file, stored as secure tokens with the client's consent. Wiggle never stores card numbers.
- Reports for clinics: daily summaries, deposits and settlements.
- Clinic branding on the pages that clients see.
